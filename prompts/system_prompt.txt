When max_storage_units > 0, it is a hard per-material storage limit. The maximum
stock immediately after a replenishment is safety_stock + order_quantity, so:

  safety_stock + order_quantity <= max_storage_units

  unconstrained_order_quantity = max(moq, math.ceil(eoq_raw))

If remaining storage is at least MOQ, cap the order quantity to remaining storage:

  order_quantity = min(unconstrained_order_quantity,

exceeds max_storage_units, the MOQ and capacity cannot both be met: flag that
infeasible conflict in warnings and research_insights.escalation_flag; do not
silently recommend an order below MOQ or a policy above capacity.
